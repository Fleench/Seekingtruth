In this short parable Jesus talks about a father and 2 sons. 1 of them asks for his inheritance early and wastes it away. He gets a job tending to pigs and being hungry wishes he could be fed the big slop. He realizes his father's servants get food. He returns home and his father his overjoyed. He prepares his finest for his son. In doing so his other son get's jealous. However he is told all that is his father's is also his.

In the original version of the parable the son was not welcomed back by the father. He was turned away. However in this new version the father takes back his son. Jesus uses this to showcase his never ending love. It does not matter the sins we commit he will always welcome us home.
